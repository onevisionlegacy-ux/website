NIK NAZRIN

Managing Director

Nik Nazrin Hazran is the Founder and Managing Director of One Vision Legacy (OVL), a dynamic Islamic financial advisory agency driven by a vision to become a world-class financial consulting organization. Through financial education, exceptional service, and leadership development, he is committed to empowering individuals, families, and communities to achieve greater financial confidence and long-term success.

Believing that true success extends beyond financial achievement, he has built OVL on the principles of knowledge, integrity, personal growth, and Islamic values. His mission is to develop future leaders, inspire individuals to become the best version of themselves, and build a lasting legacy that creates meaningful impact for generations to come.
